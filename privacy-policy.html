Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Cube Earth ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide to us through our website.

1. Who We Are

Cube Earth is a satellite-based field intelligence platform for Irish agriculture. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at saumitra@cubed-earth.com.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect personal data that you voluntarily provide when you contact us directly, for example by email. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your organisation (if provided)
  • Any other information you choose to include in your message

Our live map and dashboard can be browsed without creating an account or submitting any personal data. We do not currently require sign-up to view the parcel map or sample field reports.

3. How We Use Your Data

Information you send us is used only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Communicate with you regarding your enquiry or any follow-up discussion you request

We do not use your data for marketing purposes, and we do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

4. Legal Basis for Processing Your Data

We process your personal data based on your consent, given when you choose to contact us. You may withdraw this consent at any time by emailing the address above.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry, or as required by law. Once your enquiry has been resolved, we will delete your information unless you have asked for ongoing contact.

6. Data Sharing and Transfers

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except where required by law.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, use, or disclosure. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission)

To exercise your rights, contact us at the email address above.

9. Cookies and Tracking

We do not currently use cookies or tracking technologies to collect personal data on our website.

10. Satellite and Parcel Data

Crop classification, confidence scores, and LPIS-comparison data shown on this site are derived from publicly available satellite imagery (ESA Sentinel-2, NASA/USGS Landsat) and Ireland's LPIS 2024 open dataset (DAFM). This data describes agricultural land parcels, not individuals, and is not personal data under GDPR.

11. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Please check this page periodically. Changes take effect immediately upon posting.

Thank you for trusting Cube Earth. Your privacy is important to us.
